Daily affirmations you turn like pages. The turn asks you to slow down enough to read the line, and the line is what stays with you.

Free to download on the App Store and Google Play.

The page follows your thumb, bends, and throws a shadow across the paper as it goes over. Flick it and it settles on the other side, and the next page is waiting. The cover opens in about a second.

IThe Library

Which book doyou need today?

The library is a row of clothbound volumes, each in its own cloth and color. You pull down the one you need: Self-Kindness on a hard morning, Calm on a loud day, Confidence before the thing you are dreading. New volumes are bound each season.

Today's Reading, My Favorites and My Own Words sit on the same shelf.

IIBindings

Bound in thecloth you choose.

Cloth covers and papers, previewed on your own book before you keep one. The cloth, the paper tone and the type stay together, so the book on your screen looks like a book you would keep on a shelf.

The Bindery binds a cover from a photograph of your own.

IIIMy Own Words

Write yourown pages.

A page you wrote sits beside the written ones, in the same type, on the same paper, with your name under it if you want one. Shelve it into the volumes it belongs to and it comes back to you on an ordinary morning.

It will appear in My Own Words, and in your daily readings.

IVFavorites

Keep the pagesthat land.

The heart keeps a page. Search finds a half-remembered line by its words. The days you showed up are counted quietly. A page you hide leaves every surface, including your readings and your home screen.

VReadings

Reminders at thehours you keep.

Set a reading and the book opens itself, softly. A page before the phone starts talking, one at lunch, a last one before the light goes out. Each reading keeps its own window, its own days and its own volume, so a busy week does not end the practice.

Every notification is composed on your device.

VIHome widgets

Today’s page, onyour home screen.

The open spread, or the closed book, drawn in the binding you chose. The first page of the day is there before you open anything.

VIIYour pages

No account.Nothing uploaded.

No email, no password, no analytics, no advertising. The pages you write, the ones you keep and the hours you set stay on your device, and the whole book reads on a plane.
